How I workThe method

Four steps. Every engagement.

Whether it's an ISO 27001 build, a vendor review or an SSO rollout, the work runs in the same order. Skipping a step is usually where programs stall.

  1. 01

    Start with the business

    What can't stop, who gets hurt if it does, and who owns the decision. A hospital and an exchange house have different worst days.

  2. 02

    Map once, comply many times

    One unified control framework across ISO 27001, ADHICS and NIST CSF, so one control answers several auditors instead of three spreadsheets.

  3. 03

    Build the control with its evidence

    A control nobody can prove is an opinion. I write the configuration, the owner and the evidence at the same time.

  4. 04

    Hand over a decision, not a report

    Options, cost, residual risk and a named owner. Leadership should be able to say yes or no in one meeting.

What I'm buildingSide project · in development

NOVA SpaceBridge

Security skills are learned by doing, but most people only get to do it on the job. NOVA SpaceBridge is a simulation platform I'm building to close that gap: realistic scenarios instead of slide decks, for the people entering security, the people hiring them, the teams growing them, and the leaders who need to see where they stand.

For newcomers

NOVA Ready

Career-changers and graduates build practical skill by working through simulated incidents, audits and reviews, not just reading about them.

For hiring managers

NOVA Hire

The hiring manager sets a scenario, HR runs it with candidates, and NOVA returns the top five performers based on how they actually worked it.

For teams

NOVA Advance

Upskilling within security teams and across departments, so the people around security understand the part they play.

For CISOs & boards

NOVA Assure

One view of process, people, technology, data and data flows, with the security controls each gap calls for. This is where my GRC engineering work is heading: control-centric, evidence-backed, mapped once.

Built in the evenings with Claude Code. Early stage; happy to show it to anyone curious.
