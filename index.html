How I work, whatever the engagement

  1. 01

    Start with the business

    What can't stop, who gets hurt if it does, and who owns the decision. A hospital and an exchange house have different worst days.

  2. 02

    Assess what is real

    Look at what is actually configured, contracted and staffed, not only what the policy says. Every finding comes with its evidence.

  3. 03

    Deliver with the owners

    Controls, projects and teams only stick when the people who run them help shape them. I plan the work, clear blockers and keep the evidence as we go.

  4. 04

    Hand over a decision, not a report

    Options, cost, residual risk and a named owner. Leadership should be able to say yes or no in one meeting.

What I'm buildingUpdate · November 2026

NOVA SpaceBridge

Security skills are learned by doing, but most people only get to do it on the job. NOVA SpaceBridge is a simulation platform I'm building to close that gap: realistic scenarios instead of slide decks, for the people entering security, the people hiring them, the teams growing them, and the leaders who need to see where they stand.

Latest from the build
  • The Assure mapA digital twin of the organisation with seven lenses: application, network, security, data flow, risk, compliance and evidence.
  • Rehearse an incident on this boxPick any system on the map and turn it into a live drill, built from the real dependencies around it.
  • A real desktop, not a quizThe simulation desktop puts learners in front of the tools and tickets they would see on the job.
  • Hiring by evidenceNOVA Hire ranks how candidates handle the morning hand-over, and managers review the work by replay.
  • Two editions of AssureOne for a single organisation, one for consultants who run assessments across many clients.
  • Tested, and listeningThe training model has been tried with people with and without security experience. A market survey is now running with security leaders, HR teams and students.
For newcomers

NOVA Ready

Career-changers and graduates build practical skill by working through simulated incidents, audits and reviews, not just reading about them.

For hiring managers

NOVA Hire

The hiring manager sets a scenario, HR runs it with candidates, and NOVA returns the top five performers based on how they actually worked it.

For teams

NOVA Advance

Upskilling within security teams and across departments, so the people around security understand the part they play.

For CISOs & boards

NOVA Assure

One view of process, people, technology, data and data flows, with the controls each gap calls for. Control-centric, evidence-backed, mapped once.
